    Standing rules · v2

    The operating system

    Same engine in both cities. These supersede study.html.

    The daily engine (Mon–Fri, both cities)

    • 08:00–09:00 · Retrieval hour: yesterday's cards + the spaced deck. Never new content. A card is graded by recall, never by feel.
    • 09:00–13:00 · The day's Kaplan activities, 50 min on / 10 off. Every reading is backed by questions inside 24 h: module quiz the same day, re-test next morning.
    • Capacity model · The Kaplan stream budgets the block at 80% (≈3 h 15/day, activities never split). The first hour and the OFF blocks come on top of it — kaplan.html counts the stream only.
    • OFF days of the C·D·A·B·OFF cycle, 5:15–7:30 · 35 cumulative QBank questions, timed — 60% current topic, 40% closed topics. From Nov 16 the 40% always includes Ethics.
    • Saturday 10:00–13:00 · Topic quiz, checkpoint, mock or repair. No new content. Saturday absorbs the week's debt.
    • Sunday · Zero. Part of the method.

    Masterclass policy v2 (supersedes “selective”)

    • Every masterclass gets watched, in Kaplan's native order: notes → masterclass → module quiz. One calendar, no reconciling.
    • Strong-2023 topics (Quant · Econ · Fixed Income · Derivatives · Alternatives · Portfolio): speed and scrubbing allowed — 1.25–1.5×, and skip narration that only restates the notes.
    • Never scrub: worked examples, calculator work, and anything labelled a CFA Institute example. That material is not in the SchweserNotes.
    • FSA, the Equity valuation block (R43–46), R11 (Financial Data Science) and Ethics Application: 1×, no scrubbing.
    • The real filter is not the 3–4-question module quiz taken minutes after reading — that measures fluency. It is the next-morning re-test and the cumulative QBank. A scrubbed module that fails there gets re-watched in full.
    • Anything that exists only in the video becomes a flashcard on the spot.
    • Review phase: re-watching is a repair tool, released by the error log only — the mix stays ≈90:10 questions to video.

    Weight vs volume — where the hours actually go

    • The Kaplan stream is sized by content volume, not by exam weight. The gap is real: Quant takes 18% of stream hours for 7.5% of the exam (ratio 2.42); Ethics takes 5.8% for 17.5% (ratio 0.33). A 7× spread.
    • Therefore scrubbing is targeted, not general: Quant first (33 modules, 22 h of video), then Economics. FSA stays at 1× despite its 1.53 — it was the <50% topic, and there the volume is earned.
    • Reclaimed hours are pre-assigned: they go to cumulative QBank weighted to Ethics, Alternatives and Portfolio Construction — the three lowest ratios. Never to free time, never to more video.
    • The stream table understates Ethics by design: pass 2, the weekly drip and its permanent share of the closed-topic 40% all live outside it.
    • Alternatives (0.58) and Portfolio Construction (0.71) are thin and late (Nov 28, Dec 5). Their rehearsal window is the Dec 14–18 pre-CP3 block — that is why it is cumulative QBank over R80–102 rather than free revision.

    Flashcards — the only permitted note

    • Atomic. ≤8 per module; FSA gets 12 (journal entries, IFRS × US GAAP, ratios).
    • A card is written only after you can explain the point aloud in your own words. If you can't, that's a re-study signal, not a card.
    • Wrong QBank answers become cards too — briefest possible form.
    • No transcription. Ever.

    FSA protocol — the <50% topic

    • Full masterclasses at 1×, video before quiz.
    • Exam FSA questions assume IFRS unless US GAAP is named — drill the differences from the IFRS side.
    • Exit gate: FSA Topic Quiz ≥70% before Equity (R39) opens.
    • Gate failed: that week's first hours and OFF blocks pivot to 100% FSA, a fresh FSA quiz runs the next Saturday, and Equity opens only on a pass. Slippage is funded — it consumes the Oct 26–28 wrap-up days, the Flex days and the buffer week (≈11 weekdays of absorption), never mocks, recess or Sundays.
    • The whole block runs inside Toronto — the deepest-capacity phase of the season.

    Ethics architecture — top weight, never last again

    • Pass 1: Nov 2–14, the readaptation weeks. Reading-heavy, no calculator — built for a move. Reorder the topic in the Activity Feed; the platform supports it.
    • Drip: Nov 16 → exam day. Ethics in every OFF-block mix + ~30 min each Saturday.
    • Pass 2: Feb 1–12, mornings — ≈45 min/day alongside repair, ≈7 h dedicated, on top of the drip and the QBank share.

    Mock rules — non-negotiable

    • Five full mocks in exam conditions: two 135-min sessions, real start time, approved calculator, nothing else on the desk.
    • Answer everything — there is no penalty for guessing. Flag every guess during the sit. Saturday is the sit plus a 30-min quick scan; the full debrief — every error AND every lucky guess into the log — owns the following Monday morning. Never Sunday.
    • Repair priority = weakness × topic weight, executed as SchweserPro custom quizzes built from the log.
    • Target: ≥70%, consistently. Kaplan calibrates hard; CFAI (Learning Ecosystem) is the truest ruler.
    • Last full mock Feb 20. Nothing full-length after it. Kaplan Mock 4 becomes a question bank for the Feb 15–18 repair days, not a sitting.

    Priority hierarchy — decided now, not in a December squeeze

    • Cut first: masterclass videos of strong topics. Cut never: module quizzes, cumulative QBank, mocks, either Ethics pass.
    • Debt slides to Saturday → then to the buffer week (Dec 7–11) → never into the recess, the mocks, or a Sunday.

    Recess and taper

    • Dec 24–25 off. Dec 26 optional.
    • Dec 28 – Jan 3: full recess. No Kaplan, no QBank, no desk. Optional: 15 min of phone flashcards with coffee.
    • Jan 4–8: reactivation ramp — cumulative QBank in rising volume, zero new readings. Optional refresher: masterclasses of the two weakest topics.
    • February runs at ~18–20 h/wk. Taper Feb 22–25: ≤2 h/day — error-log greatest hits, formula sheet, Ethics skim, logistics, sleep anchored to exam start time.
